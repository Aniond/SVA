# Abigail’s Relationship Tree (0.1.22)

Press **F6**, or click Abigail in the Social page. Tree cards are read-only. History and Promises tabs retain her shared history and agreed dates. Native hearts, gifts, dating and story eligibility remain unchanged.

Talk beside Abigail, then choose **Perks** beside her portrait. Rewards and changes require explicit choices; chat alone cannot grant them. Space opens a reply and Escape ends conversation. When Gemini is unavailable, authored acknowledgements keep actions usable.

- Complete a favor to unlock the root. A completed Quartz promise unlocks mineral studies. Show Quartz, Amethyst, Earth Crystal, Frozen Tear or Fire Quartz without giving it away. One new study per day; three different minerals across three days unlock a two-for-one Geode exchange.
- Complete Iron Bar and visit mine floors on two separate days to unlock preparation discussions. Five verified mine days plus personal trust unlock the Adventure fork: two Field Snacks and five Torches, or two Cherry Bombs and five Torches.
- Reach “starting to rely on you” to unlock personal themes. Complete all three favors with sufficient recovered trust for Quiet Notes: twenty game minutes and thirty energy, capped at maximum energy.
- Exchanges, supply kits and flute breaks each have a seven-day cooldown. Unused rewards do not accumulate. Kits share their cooldown across approaches. Full backpacks reject the entire reward. Exchanges never consume quest or F9-protected items.
- Changing approach takes an explicit open-ended milestone. Prepare with Abigail, then enter a mine later that day, on two different days. Safe preparation requires a weapon, food and torch; bold requires a weapon, Cherry Bomb and torch. Checks consume nothing. Return to complete the change. Cancellation has no penalty and the original approach remains until completion.
- A broken Quartz promise pauses practical help for one morning; Iron Bar for three. Repair cannot shorten this period, repeated overdue days cannot extend it, and negative trust or an unresolved overdue Iron promise also pauses assistance. Conversation, apologies and deliveries remain available. Fish is open-ended and has no abandonment penalty.

The game records milestones, studies, cooldowns and branch changes with each farm. Existing verified deliveries and mine records migrate; no past mineral studies or shared adventures are invented. Portraits come from the installed AbigailModern content pack. Gemini chooses among its existing expression mappings.

Flute breaks are available through 23:40. They perform two ordinary ten-minute clock updates once, restore energy once, and play a short flute cue. Exiting the reply cannot repeat the benefit.

## Installation

Close Stardew Valley. Copy the packaged SolaceWeather folder into the game’s Mods folder, replacing its files while retaining your config.json. Keep AbigailModern installed for the custom portraits and sprites. Launch StardewModdingAPI.exe. This release targets Stardew Valley 1.6.15 and SMAPI 4.5.2, single player on desktop.

## Player checks

Open F6 and inspect the branches and tabs at your preferred UI scale. Use Perks while beside Abigail. Try a study, exchange, kit and flute break, then repeat each to check the limit. Check promise deadlines, cooling-off dates, a prepared two-day approach change, and save/reload. Ordinary gifts and native story events should continue normally. See testing.md for automated and live evidence and remaining manual checks.

## Living memories (0.1.23)

Tree experiences now persist beyond the recent chat window. Relevant memories are selected using their subjects, events and what the farmer later said about them. Gemini can connect them to Abigail’s personality and shared history, while knowing which parts were verified events and which were conversation. Each experience group retains first/latest dates, distinct recorded days and up to four related exchanges. Recently recalled stories receive lower priority unless the current topic makes them relevant. Unsolicited recollections share the existing daily follow-up limit.

Recorded experiences cover mineral studies, exchanges, supplies, flute breaks, preparation discussions, approach choices/changes and promise history. Ordinary gift item identities and unrelated world activities are not added by this phase. Existing records migrate conservatively: no invented old conversations, mineral identities for old exchanges, kit contents from old cooldowns, or shared mine outings.
