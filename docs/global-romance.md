# Global relationships (0.2.0)

The relationship journal and conversations support Abigail, Alex, Elliott, Emily, Haley, Harvey, Leah, Maru, Penny, Sam, Sebastian and Shane. Abigail keeps her existing promises, tree, mineral studies, flute and adventure services. Those perks are not copied to other people.

## Controls

- Talk normally to a supported character. Space opens/reopens your reply; Enter sends it; Escape leaves the conversation.
- **Bond** opens explicit choices: friendship, interest, commitments, activities and repair. Saying something in free-form chat does not accept a date, give away an item or change exclusivity.
- **F6** opens the character relationship journal. Select a person and scroll to read. Abigail's tree has its own button. The Social page also links here.
- The legacy settings `EnableAbigailMemory`, `EnableAbigailAi`, `AbigailTalkKey` and `AbigailMemoryKey` now control the shared system. Turning off AI retains authored activity dialogue. Existing weather settings remain separate.

## Growing a bond

Choose **Friendship only** or **Interested in something more**. An expression of interest alone is neither mutual attraction nor a commitment. Friendship retains shared time and lasting memories.

Dating becomes available after 28 observed days from first contact, interaction on twelve different days, and four completed activities of at least two kinds. Choosing exclusive dating updates Stardew's dating status without adding heart points. An established couple needs 28 dating days and four romantic dates. Proposal readiness needs 56 dating days, eight romantic dates and fourteen conflict-free days. Unresolved commitments can pause advancement.

Hearts, ordinary gifts and native story eligibility remain in the game. Existing partners are imported without inventing old dates or shared activities. They must explicitly reaffirm exclusivity before new romantic-boundary consequences apply.

## Shared activities

Plan a town walk, quiet time at the mountain lake, or conversation at the saloon. The game offers available times within seven days, avoiding work, clinic visits and festivals. Forecasts and future schedules can change; the appointment is checked again on the day. Outdoor bad weather or a changed schedule cancels it without blame.

One appointment can be outstanding across the farm. The normal quest journal and F6 show who, where and when. Reach the named location within thirty minutes of the agreed time; choose **Begin our meeting** when the prompt appears. Leave an open tile beside the farmer.

The real character joins a controlled scene. Three explicit choices each spend twenty game minutes. Finishing all three records a shared activity; passing through the location or leaving early does not. Normal world time advances during those minutes. No extra items, energy or stat rewards are granted. Completion credit is limited to once per person per day.

Cancel or choose another date before the appointment without penalty. A missed appointment records disappointment once. Discuss it before making another plan with that person; a missed date is not romantic betrayal.

## Boundaries and repair

Ordinary conversation, friendships and normal gifts are never betrayal. Explicit romantic actions with someone else can matter after exclusivity is agreed. Private interest is not public knowledge. A social character must be awake, nearby and able to see the action to witness it. Reports spread only through nearby character encounters, retain their original source, expire after seven days and travel at most two steps. Repeated reports do not multiply a consequence.

A first known violation allows a confrontation and repair. An unread warning is sent as a letter the next morning; it counts as delivered when opened or discussed. A second distinct violation after the warning starts fourteen days apart. Romantic plans and commitment advancement pause; ordinary conversation and designated repair activities remain available.

Repair requires an explicit agreement, two repair activities on different days and seven days respecting the boundary. Apologies alone do not complete it. Separation lasts the full fourteen days; further violations restart the seven-day requirement without extending the deadline. Failed repair ends dating or proceeds toward divorce. Shared memories remain afterward.

## Engagement and marriage

An explicit proposal checks readiness, the first farmhouse upgrade and an unprotected Mermaid's Pendant in the backpack. Acceptance consumes one pendant and uses the native wedding schedule. An engagement can be explicitly cancelled without a divorce.

During separation, household membership remains while romance and spouse assistance pause. Divorce cleanup occurs overnight through the native game. No new fee is charged, and the player keeps children, farm, money and inventory. A pending birth or adoption must finish before divorce cleanup. Native divorce status and its normal relationship effects remain.
