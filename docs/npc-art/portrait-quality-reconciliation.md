# Registered portrait quality reconciliation

Snapshot: source `AbigailModern` **0.7.91**, 150 registered `Portraits/*` sheets, 1,019 native 64×64 cells. This is a read-only artwork review. No production files, game processes or builds were changed. Mermaid's already-staged cleanup was excluded from new findings. WeatherPresenter was not registered in this snapshot and remains in the parent's integration queue.

**Confirmed: crop contamination in seven registered files, affecting 16 cells.** The strongest defect is Mrs. Raccoon's four lower expressions: a strip of the preceding bust appears across the top of each cell. All sheet dimensions match the registry. No occupied native cell became completely empty.

Cell numbering below is zero-based, left-to-right, top-to-bottom. Local rectangles are `(x,y,width,height)` within a 64×64 cell. Component counts use alpha above 30 to avoid treating nearly invisible resampling pixels as substantive fragments; they are evidence measurements, not instructions to delete every disconnected component.

## Geometry, expression occupancy and false positives

The scan decoded all 150 registered PNGs. Every actual width/height equals its registry declaration, and every dimension is divisible by 64. One-cell native sheets such as Gunther, Marlon and Dwarf are valid; they do not need to be enlarged to six expressions simply because newer special portraits have six.

There were 100 matching authoritative native portrait PNGs available for occupancy comparison. Neither of the two fully transparent registered cells is a missing required expression: Krobus cell 9 and SafariGuy cell 3 both match the original bytes exactly. The other 50 added/custom portrait sheets contain no fully transparent cell. This verifies nonempty storage, not the correctness of every dialogue expression index or facial performance.

[Placeholder proof](/C:/Users/david/SDV/artifacts/npc-modern/work/PortraitQualityReconciliation/placeholder-proof.json) confirms that the conspicuous solid-color cells in Henchman 3, Birdie 3, Marnie 5, Jodi 5, Pam 5 and Jas_Winter 5 are byte-identical native placeholders. In particular, Henchman's purple block is RGBA `(181,23,253,255)` and is **not** failed magenta keying. These cells must be preserved.

Jas_Winter's 1,133 near-magenta candidates belong primarily to her pink winter clothing; Pam's 350 belong to her pink blouse/eye makeup. UpgradeParrot's 253 belong to its deliberately purple crest. Visual review rejects those color counts as defect reports. Pink/purple hair highlights, blush, feather colors, tears, sparkles and the Governor's nausea marks are likewise intentional art, not automatically stray components.

IslandTrader and DesertTrader retain some exact RGB `(255,0,255)` pixels at very low alpha: maximum alpha 22 and 20 respectively. Their [IslandTrader](/C:/Users/david/SDV/artifacts/npc-modern/work/PortraitQualityReconciliation/IslandTrader-cells.png) and [DesertTrader](/C:/Users/david/SDV/artifacts/npc-modern/work/PortraitQualityReconciliation/DesertTrader-cells.png) previews on a light backdrop show ordinary soft edge fringes; this review does **not** promote those low-alpha counts into an urgent visible magenta-background defect. The raw counts remain in the component report for a future alpha cleanup if desired. No large opaque key-background leak was visually confirmed outside the deliberate native placeholders.

All 150 sheets were visually reviewed through these pages; selected candidates were additionally reviewed as individually separated 6×/8× cells on a light background.
- Reproduction scripts `scan.mjs`, `details.mjs` and `evidence.mjs` in the same folder only read production assets and write local audit evidence. They never key or modify production artwork.

This is a source-image quality assessment, not an installed asset-loader test or full scene playback. It does not certify every expression against all native dialogue strings, nor infer missing portraits from unused sprite-atlas space. The report intentionally keeps confirmed visible crop fragments separate from normal alpha, native placeholder cells and unresolved tiny edge details.
