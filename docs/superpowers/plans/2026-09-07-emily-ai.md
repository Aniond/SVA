# Emily fashion and creative companionship implementation plan

**Goal:** Give Emily a distinct fashion-centered personality, verified shared creative history and milestone progression, extending later into real tailoring.

**Architecture:** Follow the existing NPC-owned lifecycle services and shared memory, fashion, phone and relationship UI. Add Emily-owned save state and deterministic actions; keep garment creation/effects separate from dialogue. Use the fresh combined Solace 0.4 source before shared-file edits.

**Constraints:** Single-player; no live checkout/config/save edits or push. The integration owner owns artwork, release packaging and the game slot. Spiritual beliefs remain attributed beliefs. Explicit choices alone accept plans, transfer materials, complete activities or craft items. Friendship never implies romantic consent. Unknown clothes have neutral fashion metadata. User clarification authorizes a new Gemini fabric image per custom order and bounded paid validation.

## Early design

Emily is warmly enthusiastic, perceptive, playful and creatively independent. Fashion, fabric, color and movement are central interests. She can disagree without condescension or pushing spiritual beliefs. Her shared personal memory supports attributed preferences, corrections and follow-ups; phone knows only prior outfit observations. Do not borrow Haley's valley-girl voice or Abigail's supernatural outings.

The first cloth promise requires an actual observed Cloth item. Accept one or three days, extend once, decline or cancel, and explicitly transfer one eligible cloth. No protected item, ordinary gift reward or invented garment.

The first activity is a Town outfit-design session at 11 am, with 30-minute arrival grace and completion by noon. Three actual ten-minute choices explore a quiet/playful mood, natural/geometric pattern and finish the discussion. A dated outfit snapshot grounds the conversation. The result is a shared design idea, not a fabricated completed garment. Native schedule/weather/reservation checks and movement restoration follow the existing outing model.

Six early milestones: Made With Care (cloth or session); A Pattern Between Us (first session, repeats after three days); Wear What Feels Like You (two distinct outfits actually observed on different days, explicit style advice); Room To Be Yourself (cloth, session and three talk days); Find Your Rhythm (two sessions plus personal milestone, explicit twenty-minute movement break); Carry A Little Light (actual completed movement break, bounded weekly energy recovery on later breaks). Dance animation must be verified from native data before it can count as observed dance.

## Later tailoring — implemented reviewable defaults

Keep early branches. Proposed Made For You unlock: cloth + two sessions + three talk days, real plain wearable recipes. Proposed Care In Every Stitch: two crafted pieces on separate days plus one actual movement break, regenerative variants. Proposed costs: plain three Cloth/twenty Fiber; jacket adds one Jade, trousers adds one Aquamarine. Shared three-day craft cooldown, explicit material/recipe confirmation, stable authored item IDs and validated catalog only.

Proposed worn effects: jacket one health per sixty active-play seconds, twenty/day; trousers two energy per sixty seconds, forty/day. No pause/menu/offline/dead accumulation, no stacking, ten-second post-damage delay; unequip resets partial progress; per-farmer daily caps persist. The user confirmed NEW image generation per order. These conservative deterministic limits are implemented for review; Gemini controls neither recipes nor stats. A fixed native garment cut receives the generated fabric pattern and color.
