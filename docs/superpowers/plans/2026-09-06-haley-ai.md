## First playable design

Haley stays candid, style-conscious and observant. Early conversations can be dismissive or guarded without becoming randomly cruel; trust brings more thoughtful attention, humor and vulnerability. Friendship-only remains a complete relationship path. Photography is an interest, not a phrase inserted into every reply. Do not expose unseen heart events or assume a relationship from gifts.

User voice direction: light, natural valley-girl inflection across dialogue, texting and chatter. Casual expressive phrasing and occasional "like" or "seriously" are allowed sparingly. Avoid constant filler, exaggerated parody, or reducing her intelligence and interests to fashion or meanness. Validate low/high friendship, disagreement and remembered-outing samples.

Reuse the existing independent personal-detail store, corrections, bounded conversation history and shared-experience selection. Give Haley richer authored prompt grounding and a dedicated context for supported promises, reminders and the current outing. A farmer's report remains attributed speech. The system never converts a claimed photo session, delivery or invitation into an actual event.

First promise: bring a sunflower for a photography still life, offered only after the game has observed the player carrying one. Accept tomorrow or in three days, decline, extend once, deliver explicitly in person, or admit inability to finish. Select and transfer exactly one eligible inventory item through the existing protected-item conventions. Timely, late and abandoned outcomes affect only Haley's bounded reliability memory; no heart, money or relationship punishment. No free test items in production.

First outing: an afternoon Town photo walk. Offer the next verified free 5 pm slot with 30-minute arrival grace, requiring explicit acceptance. Meet Haley at a verified open Town location, then talk to begin. Three deliberate ten-minute choices select a setting, compose a shot, and finish the session. A camera flash indicates the final observed action; no generated image or inventory photo is promised. Weather, schedules, events and other reserved activities can make the slot unavailable. Leave/miss/reschedule without fictional completion. Only actual completed steps become Haley's shared memories.

Phone uses the same personality and known history but cannot accept, deliver, or perform promises/outings. One permitted reminder is selected from personal follow-ups or pending promises and shared across phone/in-person channels; no repeated nagging. Public town chatter continues to use public facts only.
